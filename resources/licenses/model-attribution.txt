- **Model license — status: AMBIGUOUS, needs upstream confirmation.**
  - The `rhasspy/piper-voices` HuggingFace repo declares `license: mit` in its

- **Training dataset/source**: MycroftAI/mimic3-voices (CC BY-SA 4.0); the
  voice is finetuned from the lessac medium checkpoint, itself trained on
  LibriVox recordings (public domain audiobooks).
- **Attribution requirements (if CC BY-SA 4.0 governs)**: credit the licensor
  and indicate changes made to the material.
- **Redistribution requirements (if CC BY-SA 4.0 governs)**: ShareAlike —
  adapted material must be distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license,
  which would impose share-alike obligations on any redistribution of the
  voice model. The Piper executable MIT license does not extend to voice
  model weights or training data.

**Decision required before public release**: either (a) obtain explicit
confirmation from upstream of the exact license governing
`en_US-amy-medium` and document it here, including any model-specific legal
file in the portable ZIP, or (b) switch to a voice with clearly documented
redistribution rights (e.g. a LibriVox-derived voice such as
`en_US-lessac-medium`, whose training data is public domain) before
publishing binary releases.
